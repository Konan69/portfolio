Konan Executor privacy policy

Konan Executor is a personal tool run by Konan Mualim. It connects Konan's own Google accounts to his own agents on his own machine.

It reads and writes Gmail, Calendar and Google Workspace data only for the signed-in account owner, at that owner's request. The data stays on the owner's machine. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access, remove "Konan Executor" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: kixeyems0@gmail.com.
